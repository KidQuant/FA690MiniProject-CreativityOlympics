# Mini Project: Creativity Olympics with Generative AI

## Project Overview
Welcome to the Creativity Olympics with GenAI! This project invites you to explore and showcase innovative applications of generative AI tools in any domain that excites you. Push the boundaries of what's possible with these emerging technologies and demonstrate your creative vision in a collaborative, competitive environment.

Working with your group members, you'll develop a creative application using generative AI tools in whatever way inspires you. The only technical requirement is to incorporate GenAI tools (such as DALL-E, Claude, Suno, Runway, etc.) into your project—how you use them is entirely up to you!

## Deliverables
- A 10-minute live demonstration during our Zoom lecture on May 1, 2025
- The presentation format and content are yours to design—showcase your work in whatever way best highlights your creation

## Voting
The grade for this project depends on the vote of everyone in the class. We will create a quiz to collect your vote. For each project, you need to vote for “Thumbs up” or “Neutral”. Do vote “Thumbs up” for your own group because the final thumbs-ups for your group will minus the number of people in your group to maintain fairness given varying numbers of group members.

## Grading
Total Grade consists of 50% Demo + 50% Participation

  - Demo: The demo grade is determined by peer voting (thumbs up or thumbs down). It is calculated as: (Total thumbs up – number of people in the corresponding group) ÷ (Total number of students in class - number of people in the corresponding group).
  - Participation Grade: Everyone must participate in the lecture and vote. The voting will only be available during the lecture to enforce participation. Save the date, May 1! You earn 50% of the grade by voting!
